# Phase 6.5 specification notes

Source: `/home/ubuntu/upload/Pasted_content_37.txt` supplied by the user.

Phase 6.5 must provide Admin-facing referral and growth analytics over authoritative Phase 6.1–6.4 records. Analytics must not become a second source of truth and must not mutate referral/reward state except through explicit, permission-protected Admin review actions.

Required analytics include overview, referral funnel, attribution-to-qualification and qualification-to-reward conversion, paid referral conversion where authoritative order/payment records support it, top referrers ranked by qualified referrals/paid conversions/rewards, qualification states, reward counts and values by separate units, limit-hit statistics, growth metrics, cross-source reward provenance for REFERRAL/MISSION/PROMO/ADMIN, campaign summaries, risk summaries, and bounded time series. Supported periods are today, yesterday, last 7 days, last 30 days, this month, previous month, all time, and custom range, using configured business timezone. No fabricated stages or unavailable attribution may be shown.

Risk monitoring must use privacy-safe server-side business data and existing Phase 6.2 semantics: `first_seen_at` is the application first-observed time, not Telegram account creation. Signals include referral/qualification/reward/invalid-attempt velocity, self-referral, duplicate attribution attempts, short first-seen age, rapid trial activation, reward-limit hits, promo anomalies, mission anomalies, and repeated review history. One weak signal must not prove fraud. Combined signals should prefer REVIEW_REQUIRED or HOLD_REWARD over destructive global bans.

Risk observations should be durable, deduplicated by source/event identity, policy-revisioned, and store only safe metadata. Risk levels are LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL and actions include ALLOW, OBSERVE, REVIEW_REQUIRED, HOLD_REWARD, and feature-specific REFERRAL_REWARD_BLOCK. Feature-specific blocking must not disable paid VPN, existing paid keys, wallet spending, orders, or normal access. Default auto referral blocking is OFF; auto review and auto hold may be ON. Admin review can approve, keep pending, reject, release a held reward through the existing idempotent reward engine, block/unblock referral rewards, and add internal notes. Every mutation must be permission-protected and audited.

Performance requirements are bounded/indexed queries, aggregation, pagination, and no uncontrolled full-table scans on every callback. Privacy requirements prohibit IMEI/MAC/contact scraping/device fingerprinting/IP profiling/fabricated Telegram account age. Customer UI must never expose exact risk scores or internal thresholds. Phase 6.6 is final integration only and must not be implemented in Phase 6.5.
